LLM Reasoning

Multi-Strategy Extraction

The Extraction Agent cascades through methods: intercepting Next.js data props, falling back to DOM parsing, and escalating to headless browser rendering.

🛡️

Self-Healing Pipelines

When extraction fails or anomalies are detected, the Recovery Agent formulates a new approach dynamically, preventing silent failures and alerting human operators.
